Working Paper · 2026
Geopolitical Risk, Climate Change Readiness,
and FDI Attraction in Selected Southeast Asian Countries: Evidence from a Quantile Regression Approach
Panel Data Analysis · ASEAN-6 · 1995–2023
Data N = 168 obs.
Method Pooled OLS + QR (τ=0.05–0.95)
Software Stata 17
Institution VNU–UEB · Group 47
Abstract

This study examines the joint impact of Geopolitical Risk (GPR) and Climate Change Readiness (ND-GAIN Readiness Index) on FDI inflows across six ASEAN economies — Vietnam, Singapore, Malaysia, Thailand, Indonesia, and the Philippines — over 1995–2023. Employing Pooled OLS with country-clustered robust standard errors and Quantile Regression at eleven percentile points (τ ∈ {0.05–0.95}; bootstrap SE, 1,000 replications), we confirm that GPR significantly deters FDI (coef. = −0.019, p = 0.050) while ND-GAIN Readiness significantly attracts FDI (coef. = +6.680, p = 0.016). Quantile analysis reveals heterogeneous, stage-dependent effects: GPR deterrence peaks in the Q10–Q70 "vulnerability zone" where Vietnam is empirically situated, while the ND-GAIN resilience premium accelerates monotonically to +12.07 at Q95. Results support all three hypotheses and provide evidence-based dual policy levers for Vietnam.

About Me

I am Nguyễn Quốc Khánh, a final-year student at the Faculty of International Business & Economics, Vietnam National University – University of Economics & Business (VNU-UEB), Hanoi.

My academic focus lies at the intersection of applied econometrics, international investment, and climate-finance policy. I am particularly interested in how quantitative methods can uncover heterogeneous, distributional dynamics that standard mean-based models overlook.

I am actively seeking opportunities in Data Analysis (DA) and Research & Development (R&D) roles — especially in policy institutions, consulting firms, or tech-driven research environments where rigorous analytical thinking creates real-world impact.
